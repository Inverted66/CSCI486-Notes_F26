<u> Simpsons' Paradox </u>

Berkeley:
- Men: 44% accepted out of 8442 Applicants
- Women: 35% accepted out of 4321 Applicants

Is Berkeley sexist?

When separated by department: 4 of 6 departments admitted women at a higher rate, other two had a smaller difference.
Why? female applicants tended to apply to more selective departments.
